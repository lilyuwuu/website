Home

Hi, I'm lily and this is my personal website! I mostly use this as a dumping ground for documentation I've written. If you're looking for something in particular, try the navigation bar/menu.

Things I do

I do things, primarily on the Internet. Here are some of them:

3DS modding

I've been pretty directly involved in the 3DS homebrew/modding scene since around early 2017 (over nine years!), primarily by helping people mod their consoles over at Nintendo Homebrew on Discord. To that end, I've been helping to maintain the main community text guide, 3ds.hacks.guide, since around late 2020. If you've used the guide (or even followed a YouTube guide!) to mod your 3DS in the past few years, chances are you've been following instructions I've written or contributed to! I hope you've had as much fun modding your console(s) as I've had in writing documentation that's hopefully surprisingly easy to follow. :)

My Little Pony

If you couldn't already tell from my online presence, I'm a fan of My Little Pony (or brony, or pegasister, or whatever you want to call people in the fandom). You can usually find me at conventions and meetups throughout the US and Europe, often in the games room playing or hosting pony karaoke if it's available. Beyond that, I also consume a fair bit of fandom content, primarily music and fanfic.

Links:

  • Fimfiction favourites shelf

Karaoke

I also like to do karaoke, both inside and outside of the context of My Little Pony:

  • I chart My Little Pony show/fandom songs for My Little Karaoke, a song pack and theme for UltraStar Deluxe consisting of over 800 show and fandom songs
  • I help with My Little Karaoke setups at conventions or meetups I attend by:
    • Hosting karaoke myself, with my own microphones and other equipment
    • Managing the song queue / taking requests
    • Providing resources, such as unreleased songs (primarily from my repository), updated song lists, updated programs, configuration assistance...
  • I upload the (relatively few) non-pony songs I chart to USDB, the main community resource for UltraStar TXT charts

I've also written some documentation and provided some resources about karaoke, which are listed below:

  • UltraStar Karaoke Charting / Song Creation Tutorial: a step-by-step visual tutorial for charting UltraStar TXTs
  • Running My Little Karaoke in 2026: a stopgap tutorial for running more up to date pony karaoke software
  • Pony Karaoke Unreleased Song Pack: a public repository for the pony-related songs that I've charted

Rhythm games

I like to play rhythm games, usually at the arcade! These days I normally play Ongeki, Chunithm, pop'n music and Polaris Chord, but I find most games relatively fun.

Once upon a time, I played arcade Groove Coaster quite competitively, but not so much since the end of service.

Public profiles:

  • Ongeki Score Log

Typing

Recently, I've gotten into semi-competitive typing. I practice primarily on TypeGG.

General statistics:

  • Top 10 world (among TypeGG users); #1 in Canada (technically)
    • 37,000 pp (ppv0.8)
  • Speed:
    • ~170 WPM on sustained (10,000 character) endurance quotes
    • ~190 "normalized WPM" (adjusted for quote difficulty)
    • ~200 WPM peak on random-word tests (1 minute)

Quotes I'm most proud of:

  • Best play: Linux Network Servers - List of Tables (~150 WPM for 13 minutes/9600 characters of mostly technical terms and newlines)
  • Best medium-length endurance: The sport of mountain biking (~205 WPM for 3 minutes/3100 characters)
  • Best long-term endurance: The New Science of Narcissism - Smart ways (~180 WPM for 8 minutes/7800 characters)

Public transit / smartcard collecting

I'm a fan of taking public transit wherever I go, and also of collecting public transportation smart cards ("IC cards") as a memento/souvenir of the places I visit. To date, I've collected over a hundred cards, most of them from Japan.

Resources:
